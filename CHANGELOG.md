# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.
- **Markdown preview:** ```` ```dhole ```` blocks are drawn as Divooka draws them, with Dhole 0.2.0's public build. The editor's light, dark or high-contrast theme is followed unless a diagram names its own theme or the `dhole.preview.theme` setting picks one, and each diagram's problems are listed under it.
- **Problems panel:** problems in a Markdown document's blocks and in `.dhole` files are reported at their line and column, in English or Chinese.
- **Language support:** `.dhole` files are recognised as their own language, with colouring there and inside ```` ```dhole ```` blocks, comments, brackets and indentation.
- **Playground and preview:** *Open in the Dhole Playground* appears above each block, and a `.dhole` file has a preview of its own.
